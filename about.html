THE STORY

Blessedjeff
Lil Jefry.

Lil Jefry is an upcoming artist from Juba, South Sudan, also known as “theblessedjeff.” He started making his own music at 13 using Voloco. After taking a break, he returned to music seriously, driven to take his career as far as he can. In real life, Lil Jefry is an introvert, and he makes music across multiple genres.

From Juba to everywhere, he is building a sound that feels personal, direct, and impossible to ignore.
